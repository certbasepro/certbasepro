## What it is

CertBase is a free, independent platform for practising IT certification exams. One click to start — no signup, no premium tier, no "free trial" that asks for a card.

- 📚 **130,000+ questions** across **25+ providers** — AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Cisco, CompTIA, Databricks, Snowflake, and more
- 🧠 **AI explanation on every answer** — why the right one is right, and why the others are wrong
- 📖 **Study mode** — answer a question, see the explanation immediately
- ⏱️ **Quiz mode** — simulate exam conditions, get your results at the end
- 💬 **A discussion thread on every question** — debate answers, share tips, flag anything outdated

## Why free?

Quality certification prep shouldn't cost money. Practice questions are the highest-value thing you can do before an exam — and charging people who are already paying exam fees (students, career-switchers, people in lower-income regions) never sat right with us.

So CertBase is free, permanently. No investors steering it, and no training vendor it has to sell for. We're a small independent project, and that independence is the point.

## Built by people who've passed these exams

Every question can be reviewed, discussed, and improved by people who've actually sat the exam. If you've passed one, you can give back:

- ✍️ **Submit questions** for an exam you've passed
- ✅ **Review and improve** existing questions
- 💬 **Help moderate** discussions and flag outdated content

→ **[Contribute at certbase.pro/contribute](https://certbase.pro/contribute)** — contributors get acknowledged for what they help build.
